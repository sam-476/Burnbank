          How Burnbank works

          You start at 0 and earn as the day goes

          Every hour, on the hour, you earn about 100 kcal (your daily number ÷ 24; change it in Menu). At 1pm you've earned 1,300. Waiting pays. Don't know your daily number? Menu → Calculate works it out from your height, weight, age and activity.

          Food takes, exercise gives

          Type the calories, tap Add. For exercise pick Walk, Run, Gym and so on, type the minutes, and those calories come back.

          Red is fine

          Ate more than you've earned? The balance goes red. It climbs back on its own, hour by hour. No nagging.

          Midnight

          The day closes at whatever you finished on and goes into Total saved. Next morning the app shows what you banked. A day only counts if you logged something, so on a fasted day log 0.

          Goal, no scale

          Set the weight you want to lose. 1 kg of fat is about 7,700 kcal, so Total saved becomes a progress bar and a weight estimate. Scales lie day to day; this doesn't.

          Macros and supplements

          Tick "Advanced" when adding food to enter protein (carbs and fat if you like). The Macros tab shows today's totals and lets you tick the supplements you took. None of it changes your balance.

          Little things

          Tap an entry to fix it. "Log to yesterday" for last night's dinner. "Save as favourite" makes a one-tap button; hold a favourite to remove it. Themes, reminders and backup live in Menu.

          Moving from the old Burnbank

          Old app: Menu → Copy backup. This app: paste it on the first screen, or Menu → Restore. Then uninstall the old one.

          Your data

          Stays on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
